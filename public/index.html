Before you go

  • Bring a valid government-issued ID and your reference number.
  • Representatives must bring an authorization letter signed by the patient and representative, plus their valid IDs.
  • Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your OPD appointment.
  • Requested documents may be claimed three (3) days after the date of request, or on the next working day if that falls on a weekend or holiday.
